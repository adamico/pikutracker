# Custom Instruments are unlimited in the Project and assigned to SFX 0–7 at Export
_Partly superseded by [ADR 0025](0025-at-most-8-custom-instruments.md): a Project holds at most 8 Custom Instruments, numbered 8 to F with one digit, and Custom Instrument 8 + k always goes to SFX k instead of a slot chosen at Export._

A Step's instrument is two hex digits: 00–07 are PICO-8's Built-in Instruments, and 08–FF are Custom Instruments (SFX Instruments or Waveform Instruments). A Custom Instrument has no fixed SFX number in the Project. Export gives each Custom Instrument used by the 4 Exported Tracks a free SFX in 0–7, within the Project's claimed SFX range, and rewrites the Steps' instrument digits to match. We chose this over mirroring PICO-8's 8 fixed slots because it follows ADR 0001: the Project holds more than a Cart can, just as 8 Tracks are Exported as 4 channels, and only what is Exported must fit.

## Consequences

- An Export that uses more than 8 Custom Instruments, or whose claim does not cover enough of SFX 0–7, fails. There is no manual pinning of an instrument to a slot in the MVP.
- Cart Import turns every SFX 0–7 that some note uses as an instrument into a Custom Instrument.
- Filters live on each Phrase, like Speed, because PICO-8 sets them per SFX. A Custom Instrument has its own filters, which get added for each note that uses it, as in PICO-8.
- A Waveform Instrument holds its 64 samples, Bass, DETUNE, REVERB and DAMPEN, and nothing else. Speed, loop, NOIZ and BUZZ have no effect on one, so the editor doesn't show them and Cart Import discards them. Export writes a fixed header: byte 65 is 16, or 17 with Bass, byte 66 is `80` and byte 67 is `00` (amended by [Apply the PICO-8 check results to earlier decisions](https://github.com/adamico/pikutracker/issues/28)).
- Export gives the Custom Instruments slots in order of their number, each taking the lowest claimed SFX 0–7 still free, and the Phrases' SFX fill the remaining claimed slots in order of first use. Only Steps inside LEN need a slot, and of those only audible ones, unless the Phrase has REVERB. A Step that plays a Custom Instrument the Project doesn't define still gets one, holding an empty SFX, so that it plays silence as it does in the tracker.
- Under REVERB, a silent Step that names a Custom Instrument needs that instrument's slot too. Real PICO-8 0.2.7 runs the waves on through a silent custom note as its instrument would play them, whatever the note's digit. An SFX Instrument plays through its notes at its own speed, a Waveform Instrument runs as a plain wave at the note's pitch, and an empty slot runs as the triangle at pitch 0. The SFX's own DETUNE applies. The synth does the same, so a silent Step sounds the same in the tracker and in the Exported Cart ([Measure two Export cases near silence on real PICO-8](https://github.com/adamico/pikutracker/issues/63)).
