# Chain rows play in lockstep, one Pattern each
_Partly superseded by [ADR 0022](0022-phrases-loop-as-sfx-do.md): the loop moved from Fill on the Chain entry to the Phrase loop._

Within a Song row, the k-th entry of every Track's Chain plays together as a **Chain row**, and Export compiles each Chain row into exactly one Pattern. A Chain entry can be marked **Fill**, which repeats its Phrase until the Chain row ends and compiles to a looping SFX. A Chain row lasts as long as its longest Phrase that is not marked Fill. This is PICO-8's own song structure: a flat list of Patterns, each holding 4 SFX that play together, where a looping SFX repeats until the Pattern ends. We chose it over M8-style free-running Chains, which Export would have to split into SFX wherever any Track's Phrase starts or ends. Free-running uses more SFX in ways that are hard to predict, can't Export cuts that land mid-Step or mid-arpeggio-group, and doesn't round-trip through Cart Import. The cost: Chains in parallel Tracks don't run independently. A [16, 16] Chain beside a [32] Chain lasts 48 Steps, not 32.

## Consequences

- This replaces part of ADR 0003. "A Song row ends when its longest Track finishes" now applies to each Chain row. Cart Import no longer unrolls a looping SFX into repeats. A loop that covers the whole SFX becomes a Phrase marked Fill. Phrases still have no loop points; the loop belongs to the Chain entry.
- Every Phrase starts at note 0 of its own SFX, so arpeggio groups of 4 always line up with PICO-8's.
- PICO-8 ends a Pattern when its *left-most* non-looping channel ends. When a shorter Phrase without Fill sits to the left of the longest one, Export sets its SFX's loop start to the Phrase's length and its loop end to one more, so the channel stays silent and no longer decides the length. The same rule covers a 32-Step Phrase, whose loop runs from 32 to 33, past the last note. Real PICO-8 0.2.7 confirmed both cases (amended by [Apply the PICO-8 check results to earlier decisions](https://github.com/adamico/pikutracker/issues/28)).
- If every entry in a Chain row is marked Fill, the longest plays once and sets the length. PICO-8 plays a Pattern whose channels all loop for 32 × the slowest Speed. So Export writes the longest Fill entry's SFX without a loop, using LEN. That channel then sets the length, and the left-most rule above covers any shorter channel to its left (amended by [How Cart Import builds a Project](https://github.com/adamico/pikutracker/issues/11)).
- When a Chain row takes its length from a Track that isn't Exported, or has no Exported entries at all, Export puts a silent SFX on the left-most free channel to set the length. It holds the Chain row's Ticks as the fewest notes whose Speed fits in a byte. With no free channel, Export writes the Pattern anyway and warns that the Chain row will play at another length.
- The silent loop plays exactly like a channel that ends at LEN. Real PICO-8 0.2.7 renders the two the same, sample for sample, under REVERB with and without DAMPEN, and the echo keeps the SFX's DAMPEN in both. The same recordings show that PICO-8 feeds its echo with what DAMPEN leaves, so a damped echo dies away faster. The synth now does both, and keeps DAMPEN on the echo of a channel that ends at LEN, so the tracker matches ([Measure two Export cases near silence on real PICO-8](https://github.com/adamico/pikutracker/issues/63)).
